Privacy Policy

Respecting visitors and protecting information

Steven Yange Official Website respects the privacy of all visitors. This website is designed to share information about ministry, community development initiatives, projects and public activities.

We do not collect personal information from visitors unless they voluntarily provide information through email, phone communication or other contact channels.

Any information provided by visitors is used only for responding to enquiries, ministry collaboration, community development discussions and partnership opportunities.

This website may include links to external platforms such as Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn and TikTok. Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy policies of those platforms when using their services.

Steven Yange Official Website is committed to responsible communication, respect, transparency and protecting visitor privacy.
