Biography of Steven Yange

Steven Yange is a Catholic priest, community leader, and social development advocate from Pina, Wapenamanda District, Enga Province, Papua New Guinea.

He was ordained a priest on 16 December 2016 and has dedicated his life to serving God and people through faith, pastoral ministry, peace-building, and community development.

Education and Formation

Steven received his early education in Enga Province. His formation journey included St Fidelis Minor Seminary, Good Shepherd Seminary Fatima, and Catholic Theological Institute Bomana.

Vision and Service

His vision is to promote faith, unity, peace, youth empowerment, and sustainable community development.

Leadership

Founder of Nakaupi Dingi Association and founder/leader of Mt Zion Gospel Music Association, promoting hope, talent development, gospel music, and community support.
