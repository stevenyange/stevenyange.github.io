Biography of Steven Yange

Steven Yange was born in Pina, Wapenamanda District, Enga Province, Papua New Guinea. He has dedicated his life to faith, service, leadership, community development and empowering people through education, music and social initiatives.


Education Journey

1991 – Grade Preparatory
1992 – 1997 – Primary Education
1998 – 2001 – Secondary Education
2003 – 2004 – Matriculation Studies, Enga University Centre
2006 – 2007 – St Fidelis Minor Seminary
2008 – 2010 – Good Shepherd Seminary Fatima
2012 – 2014 – Catholic Theological Institute Bomana, National Capital District


Priestly Ministry

After years of formation and pastoral preparation, Steven Yange was ordained a Catholic priest on 16 December 2016. Since ordination, he has served in pastoral ministry, supporting communities through spiritual guidance, education, youth activities, peace-building and social development.


Vision and Mission

His vision is to promote faith, peace, unity, education, youth empowerment and sustainable community development. Through various initiatives, he continues to encourage young people and communities to use their gifts and resources for the benefit of society.
