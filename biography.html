Professional Profile

A journey dedicated to faith, service and community transformation.

Steven Yange is a Catholic priest, community leader and development advocate from Papua New Guinea.

He is committed to serving God and humanity through pastoral ministry, education, leadership, peace-building and community development initiatives.

Through faith-based projects and social initiatives, he encourages people, especially young people, to discover their gifts and contribute positively to society.

Priesthood & Ministry

Steven Yange was ordained a Catholic priest on 16 December 2016.

Since ordination, he has dedicated his life to pastoral ministry, evangelisation, spiritual guidance, community service and bringing hope to people through faith.
