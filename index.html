Steven Yange

Catholic Priest • Community Leader • Humanitarian • Project Developer

My Biography My Projects Contact Me

Steven Yange is a Catholic priest from Papua New Guinea, committed to serving God and humanity through faith, leadership and community development.

Through priestly ministry, community initiatives and social projects, he promotes peace, hope, youth empowerment and positive change in society.

His vision is to build communities where people are encouraged, supported and inspired to use their gifts for the benefit of others.

Ministry & Service

Priestly Ministry

Serving the Church through pastoral ministry, spiritual guidance and sharing God's message of love and hope.

Community Leadership

Supporting communities through peace-building, youth empowerment and development initiatives.

Gospel Music Ministry

Using gospel music as a tool to proclaim faith, encourage young talents and unite communities.
