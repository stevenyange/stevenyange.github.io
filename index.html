About Steven Yange

A life dedicated to faith, service and community transformation.

Steven Yange is a Catholic priest from Papua New Guinea, committed to serving God and humanity through faith, leadership and community development.

Through priestly ministry, community initiatives and social projects, he promotes peace, hope, youth empowerment and positive change in society.

His vision is to build communities where people are encouraged, supported and inspired to use their gifts for the benefit of others.
