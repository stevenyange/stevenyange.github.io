About Steven Yange

Steven Yange is a Catholic priest, community leader and humanitarian from Papua New Guinea. Through faith, leadership and community service, he works to promote peace, youth empowerment, gospel music and sustainable development. He is the founder of community initiatives including Mt Zion Gospel Music Association and Nakaupi Dingi Association.
