About Nakaupi Dingi Association

Nakaupi Dingi Association Inc. is a community-focused organisation established to support development, environmental responsibility, peace-building and empowerment initiatives.

The association works to encourage communities to participate in activities that improve livelihoods, protect natural resources and create opportunities for future generations.

Meaning of Nakaupi Dingi

"Nakaupi Dingi" represents a natural spring flowing from the mountain, a source of life that continues to provide water and hope.

The name reflects the association's commitment to becoming a source of support, opportunity and positive change for communities.

Our Mission

To empower communities through sustainable development, environmental responsibility, education, cooperation and projects that improve people's lives.

Our Vision

To build peaceful, united and self-reliant communities where people protect nature, support one another and create opportunities for future generations.

Our Focus Areas

Environmental Protection

Protecting natural resources and promoting responsible care for the environment.

Community Empowerment

Supporting people through participation, skills development and opportunities.

Sustainable Development

Promoting initiatives that improve livelihoods and community wellbeing.
