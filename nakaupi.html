Nakaupi Dingi Association

Preserving Nature • Promoting Peace • Empowering People

A community-based organisation committed to sustainable development, environmental protection, community empowerment and improving the well-being of people through positive initiatives.

Our Mission

To empower communities through sustainable development, environmental responsibility, education, cooperation and projects that improve people's lives.

Our Vision

To build peaceful, united and self-reliant communities where people protect nature, support one another and create opportunities for future generations.

Our Focus Areas

Environmental Protection

Protecting natural resources and preserving the environment.

Community Empowerment

Supporting people through skills, participation and development.

Sustainable Development

Promoting projects that improve livelihoods and community wellbeing.
