Mt Zion Gospel Music Association

A Christian Gospel Music Association dedicated to proclaiming the Good News through music, inspiring faith, promoting peace, and empowering young people to use their God-given talents for the service of the Church and the community.

View Gallery

Our Mission

To evangelise through gospel music, nurture musical talent, strengthen Christian values, promote peace and unity, and encourage young people to serve God through their gifts.

Our Vision

To become a leading Christian gospel music association in Papua New Guinea, inspiring lives through worship, faith, and community service.
