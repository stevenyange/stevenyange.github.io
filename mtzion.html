A Christian Gospel Music Association dedicated to proclaiming God's message through music, inspiring faith, promoting peace, and empowering young people to use their talents for God's glory.

About the Association

Mt Zion Gospel Music Association is a Christian organisation using gospel music as a powerful tool for evangelisation, community unity and youth empowerment.

The association encourages talented young people to develop their musical gifts and use them in service of God and society.

Our Mission

To evangelise through gospel music, nurture musical talent, strengthen Christian values, promote peace and unity, and encourage young people to serve God through their gifts.

Our Vision

To become a leading Christian gospel music association in Papua New Guinea, inspiring lives through worship, faith and community service.

Our Objectives

Faith Promotion

Share the Gospel message through Christian music and worship.

Youth Empowerment

Encourage young people to develop and use their talents positively.

Peace & Unity

Use music to promote harmony, friendship and community connection.

"Using gospel music to proclaim faith, inspire hope and transform communities."
